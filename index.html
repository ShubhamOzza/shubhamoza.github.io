Background

About

I studied BCom in Accounting and Finance, then went straight into an MBA in Finance. That combination gave me a solid grounding in how financial systems actually work: trade lifecycles, derivatives, corporate actions, and the everyday plumbing behind capital markets.

My first job was at IBM, working with a UK-based investment bank on their risk and compliance team. I spent my days reading through high-risk client portfolios, building audit trails, and catching the small things in financial data that quietly turn into bigger problems if nobody notices them in time.

From there I moved to Capgemini, working in capital markets for a US investment bank. The focus was OTC derivatives: collateral management, ISDA agreements, and margin accounts. It's the messier side of derivatives operations, where getting the details right matters most.

Then I joined Landytech, a London-based fintech, first as a Data Analyst and later as an Associate handling client delivery. I got accurate portfolio and performance data out to institutional clients, reconciled AUM across more than a billion dollars in assets, validated returns, and managed ETL processes across equities, fixed income, hedge funds, private equity, and real estate.

I came to UT Dallas to close the gap between the operational work I'd been doing and the quantitative side I'd always wanted to go deeper on. Alongside coursework, I worked as a Graduate Student Assistant at the Naveen Jindal School of Management, and I'm now working as a Financial Data Engineer at Intellilake in New York, remote, building and optimizing financial data pipelines. The projects on this site are where the rest of that shift is heading.
